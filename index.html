EST. 1933

a corner pub
that's outlasted
nearly everything.

Cindy Henry — wife of the late Mayor Tom Henry — ran the Green Frog for twenty years as Bloomingdale's unofficial mayor. Knew every regular's drink. Made strangers feel like neighbors.

She passed in January 2024. Today her daughter Elizabeth — "Mama Frog Jr." to the regulars — has the bar back in the family. Same wood. Same flat top. Same name on the door.
